Privacy Policy

Adams Global Publishers ("we," "us," "our") provides professional book publishing services, including ghostwriting, editing, formatting, cover design, ISBN registration, manuscript editing, and marketing consultation. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and handle your information when you engage our services.

This policy applies alongside our Terms & Conditions and Payment Policy, which govern website usage, client obligations, and payment terms respectively. Read all three together before engaging our services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you and through your use of our services. The categories below define exactly what we gather and why.

Personal identification information — name, email address, phone number, mailing address, and billing details, collected when you request a quote, place an order, or communicate with our team.

Project content — manuscripts, outlines, drafts, source material, author notes, and any other content you submit for ghostwriting, editing, formatting, or design services.

Payment information — billing details processed through our designated payment channels, as described in our Payment Policy. We do not store full payment card numbers on our own systems.

Communication records — emails, chat logs, revision requests, and call notes exchanged during the course of your project, retained to maintain an accurate service history.

Technical data — IP address, browser type, and site usage data collected automatically when you visit our website, used to maintain and improve site performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information serves one purpose: delivering the publishing services you've engaged us for. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Deliver contracted services — assign your project to the appropriate ghostwriter, editor, or designer and manage it through to completion.
  • Communicate project updates — send drafts, revision requests, milestone confirmations, and delivery notifications.
  • Process payments — verify and process deposits, milestone payments, and final balances in accordance with our Payment Policy.
  • Provide customer support — respond to inquiries, resolve disputes, and address revision requests.
  • Improve our services — analyze aggregated, anonymized feedback to refine our editing, design, and publishing workflows.

We do not sell your personal information or your manuscript content to third parties. Any sharing of your data is limited to the service providers named in Section 4, and only to the extent necessary to complete your project.

3. Confidentiality of Your Work

Confidentiality is a core term of every project, not an optional add-on. When you submit a manuscript or project brief, that content is treated as strictly confidential from the moment it reaches our team.

Non-disclosure as standard practice. Every ghostwriter, editor, and designer assigned to your project operates under a confidentiality obligation. Your story, concept, and personal details are not discussed, shared, or referenced outside the scope of your project.

Full ownership transferred to you. Upon final delivery and completion of payment, full ownership and intellectual property rights to the finished content transfer to you. We retain no claim to your manuscript, cover design, or published work beyond what is needed to deliver the service itself.

Limited internal access. Access to your project files is restricted to the team members directly assigned to your project — your writer, editor, or designer — and relevant project managers overseeing quality control.

4. Data Security

We apply security measures proportionate to the sensitivity of the information we hold. These measures include:

  • Encrypted storage for manuscripts, personal data, and project files, protecting them from unauthorized access.
  • Secure file-transfer channels for sending and receiving drafts, revisions, and final deliverables.
  • Access controls limiting manuscript and personal data visibility to assigned team members only.
  • Regular system audits to identify and close potential vulnerabilities before they become incidents.

No system is entirely immune to risk. If a data breach affecting your personal information occurs, we will notify you promptly and outline the steps taken to contain and remediate it.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with a limited number of third-party providers to deliver parts of our service — for example, payment processors for billing and ISBN registration bodies for registration services. Each provider is given access only to the data required to perform its specific function, and each is bound by its own confidentiality and data-protection obligations.

We do not permit third-party providers to use your manuscript content or personal data for any purpose beyond the service they are contracted to perform.

6. Originality and Plagiarism-Free Guarantee

Original content protects both your reputation as an author and your legal standing as a copyright holder. For that reason, every manuscript we deliver is checked against plagiarism-detection software before final delivery.

100% original content. All ghostwritten and edited material is written or revised specifically for your project — never recycled, resold, or reused across other client engagements.

Pre-delivery screening. Manuscripts are run through plagiarism-detection tools prior to delivery, and a report can be provided upon request.

Unlimited revisions. If any portion of the delivered content falls short of our originality standard, we will revise it at no additional cost until it meets that standard, in line with our unlimited-revisions commitment.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information and project files only as long as necessary to complete your project, satisfy legal or tax obligations, and resolve any post-delivery disputes. Manuscript files are retained for a limited period after final delivery to support revision requests, after which they are securely deleted unless you request a longer retention period in writing.

8. Your Rights

You retain specific rights over the personal data and content you share with us:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Request deletion of your personal data, subject to any ongoing contractual or legal obligations.
  • Withdraw consent for non-essential data uses, such as marketing communications.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 10.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services, legal obligations, or security practices evolve. Material changes will be communicated to active clients directly, and the revised policy will be posted on our website with an updated effective date.
